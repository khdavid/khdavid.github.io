# Privacy Policy for Calm Motion

**Last updated: October 27, 2026**

## Overview
Calm Motion is an app that helps overcome car sickness.

## Data Collection
Calm Motion uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand app usage and improve the app.
It records when motion cues are switched on and off, and how long they were active.

Google Analytics also collects information automatically, including an app-instance ID,
the Android advertising ID when available, app sessions and screen views, and approximate
location derived from a masked IP address. These identifiers can distinguish app instances
or devices; the custom events do not include a name or the route traveled.

## Third-Party Services
Analytics data is transmitted to and processed by Google. Google states that Analytics data
is encrypted in transit. For more information, see
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and its
[Google Analytics for Firebase data disclosure](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11582702).

## Your Choices
You can reset or delete your Android advertising ID in your device settings. Deleting it
prevents apps from receiving the advertising ID, but does not stop all analytics collection
or remove other identifiers or previously collected data. You can contact us with questions
or requests concerning your data.

## Children's Privacy
The analytics collection described above is not restricted by age in the app.
